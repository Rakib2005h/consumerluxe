Buying a Memory Foam Mattress Online — A Checklist

August 2026 · Consumer Luxe Editorial

A mattress-in-a-box is one of the best and worst things you can buy online: great when the spec sheet is honest, painful when it isn't. We track several memory foam mattresses with verified prices — here's the checklist we run before recommending any of them.

1. Thickness is not quality — but it matters

8-inch mattresses (like the FDW Twin, $89.99 checked Aug 2026) are fine for kids' rooms, guest rooms and bunk beds. For nightly adult use, 12–14 inch builds (like the Novilla Queen 12" at $233.99 or the Sweetnight CoolNest 14" at $469.99) usually offer better pressure relief simply because there's more foam layered above the support core.

2. Certifications that actually mean something

  • CertiPUR-US® — foam tested for harmful substances. Most reputable brands carry it (EGOHOME, Sweetnight and Novilla all do).
  • OEKO-TEX — covers textiles, a plus for covers.
  • Anything vague like "eco-friendly certified" without a named body — treat as marketing.

3. The fiberglass question

Some budget mattresses use fiberglass as a flame barrier; if the cover is unzipped, it can contaminate a room. Brands now advertise "fiberglass free" explicitly — the EGOHOME Full 14" ($469.99, checked Aug 2026) is one. If a listing doesn't say, assume nothing and ask.

4. Cooling claims: look for gel + open-cell, not adjectives

"Sleeps cool" is easy to print. Gel-infused foam, open-cell structure, graphite or phase-change covers (EGOHOME's Graphene-Cool-Tech, Sweetnight's CoolNest knit) are at least specific technologies you can compare.

5. Sleep trial, warranty, and who handles returns

Memory foam needs 2–4 weeks to fully expand and for your body to adjust. A 100-night trial is the practical minimum; check whether returns go through the brand or the platform. Amazon returns are generally simpler than third-party seller freight returns for a 60 lb box.

6. Match the mattress to the base

Memory foam needs a solid or slatted base with slats under ~4 inches apart. An 18-inch metal platform frame (like the Superay Queen, $112.97) adds storage and skips the box spring entirely.
